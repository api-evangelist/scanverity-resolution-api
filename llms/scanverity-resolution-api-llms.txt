# Scanverity Intelligence

> Independent Polymarket intelligence for market rules, resolution sources, evidence, liquidity, risk and monitoring. Scanverity shows the source, freshness and evidence status of every result. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Polymarket.

Polymarket is the currently supported prediction-market data source. Findings use the published epistemic labels Observed fact, Deterministic finding, Historical association, Modelled and Unknown; each is timestamped and carries stated limitations. Read-only research: no order execution, no custody, no trading advice.

## Seven verification products

- [Resolution Watch](https://scanverity.com/en/risk): rule language, resolution sources, ambiguity and calibrated resolution risk
- [Market Truth](https://scanverity.com/en/execution): current probability, bid/ask, order-book depth and evidence-bound execution conditions
- [Event Map](https://scanverity.com/en/events): related markets, shared dependencies, deadlines and negative-risk relationships
- [Smart Flow](https://scanverity.com/en/traders): source-backed wallet and cohort observations with explicit coverage limits
- [Shock Feed](https://scanverity.com/en/explore): material market changes separated from ordinary price movement
- [Executable Basket](https://scanverity.com/en/execution): whether an apparent opportunity survives spread, depth, slippage and fees
- [Daily Brief](https://scanverity.com/en/monitoring-overview): monitored-market changes summarized with evidence and timestamps
